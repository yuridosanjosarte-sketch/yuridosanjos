About the Artist

Yuri dos Anjos (born 1992, Brazil) is a contemporary Brazilian artist whose practice is rooted in ancestry, territory, and spiritual consciousness. Of Indigenous Tupi heritage, his work emerges from lived experience and cultural memory, transforming canvas into a space of resistance, remembrance, and transcendence. Living and working in the coastal region of São Paulo, Yuri develops a visual language that bridges the visible and the invisible. Through oil on canvas, he constructs layered compositions marked by symbolic intensity, tactile depth, and atmospheric force. His paintings are not illustrations of identity — they are embodiments of it. Each work is a singular creation, never reproduced. The materiality of oil, the density of gesture, and the deliberate construction of space create pieces that carry both emotional gravity and spiritual presence. His compositions oscillate between silence and strength, grounding and expansion, inviting the viewer into contemplative confrontation with memory and being. Yuri’s practice stands at the intersection of contemporary expression and ancestral continuity. By channeling Indigenous cosmology and personal narrative, he reclaims space within global contemporary art discourse while maintaining authenticity of origin. Every artwork is signed and issued as a one-of-a-kind piece, accompanied by a certificate of authenticity.
