About the Artist

Born in Brazil in 1992, Yuri Dos Anjos is a contemporary artist specializing in mastery-level oil painting. With a focus on realism, his work explores the depth and soul of indigenous portraiture. As a descendant of the Tupinambá people, Yuri weaves his heritage into every brushstroke, creating evocative pieces that command attention. His art has gained international recognition, reaching private collections in Switzerland.
